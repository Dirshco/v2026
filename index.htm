📋 CPT 99309 Documentation Support

  • Anticoagulation: Increased bleeding/falls risk; monitor for hemorrhage.
  • Antiarrhythmic (Amiodarone): Monitor thyroid, pulmonary, hepatic function, bradycardia.
  • Diabetes medications: Hypoglycemia risk with reduced oral intake.
  • Diuretics: Hypotension, electrolyte imbalance, dehydration, AKI risk.
  • Antihypertensives: Hypotension, hyperkalemia, angioedema, renal impairment.
  • Cardiac conditions: Exacerbation, fluid overload, arrhythmias, falls.
  • Polypharmacy: Drug interactions, adverse effects, medication errors.
  • History of falls: Heightened risk with anticoagulation and hypotensive agents.
